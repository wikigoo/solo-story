## Sci-Fi & Cyberpunk Style

### Characteristics

Science fiction-themed art in AI spans futuristic technology, space settings, and cyberpunk cityscapes. A particularly popular variant is the cyberpunk style—gritty, neon-lit urban dystopias inspired by works like *Blade Runner*. These images have high-tech elements (cyborgs, robots, holograms), moody futuristic city streets with glowing neon signs, and often a contrast between dark shadows and vibrant neon colors.

Another aspect of sci-fi art includes sleek starships, alien planets, and cosmic vistas. The general sci-fi style uses a cooler or high-contrast palette and dramatic perspectives to evoke awe or tension. In cyberpunk specifically, one finds a mix of modern noir and Japanese anime influences (think *Ghost in the Shell* or *Akira*), with characters in techwear or augmented with cybernetic parts.
